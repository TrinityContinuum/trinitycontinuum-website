Free browser-based 3D

Parametric 3D concept envelopes

Six editable 3D module envelopes, generated locally in your browser. Rotate the view, adjust dimensions, and download models for use in free CAD applications. No account, subscription, or server required.

3D model controls

Conceptual envelopes only. These are not validated equipment, piping, structural, thermal, or manufacturing designs. Do not fabricate or build from these models without qualified engineering review.
