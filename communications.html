The fourth utility · Communications

Connections that help communities keep operating.

Trinity Continuum is developing a four-utility approach: Water, Power, Thermal and Communications. The communications function is intended to coordinate system operations and support continuity needs during industrial interruptions and emergencies.

Communications services, network coverage, interoperability, failover, information security and field performance are development objectives, not verified service guarantees.

Four essential utilities · one platform

Communications connects the operating picture.

Supervisory connectivity

Proposed secure telemetry, status reporting and remote monitoring across water, electricity and thermal subsystems.

Emergency coordination

Conceptual pathways for communicating system status and supporting authorized responders and critical facilities during outages.

Resilient network planning

Site-specific architecture may evaluate terrestrial, cellular or satellite connectivity and controlled failover, subject to availability, cybersecurity and provider agreements.

Communications capabilities do not replace public emergency networks or establish telecom-carrier service. Safety-critical control must remain locally safe when remote links fail. All services require design verification, applicable licensing, permissions and acceptance testing.
