Trinity Coordination Desk

Internal corporate inbox coordination • Information for authorized mailbox users

Trinity Continuum's Coordination Desk is a private operational tool for company-approved Gmail/Google Workspace inboxes. It uses each mailbox holder's separate Google authorization to read incoming messages and new-message history, identify potential meetings, engineering and procurement inquiries, delivery exceptions and no-contact instructions, and maintain deduplicated technical receipts for authorized follow-up.

The Gmail watcher requests the gmail.readonly permission only. It never sends, deletes or modifies mailbox messages. Its automatic classification is rule-based, not an autonomous reply or decision system. Outbound communications and customer booking are separate workflows requiring their own permissions and operational checks.

The Desk is intended for named Trinity corporate users, not external customer enrollment. This page provides information about why Gmail access is requested; it is not a sign-in page and does not display or provide access to private correspondence.

Please review our privacy notice, including the Google Gmail data access and retention disclosure. For help, questions about access, or a deletion or revocation request, use Trinity's contact page. Authorization can be revoked by the mailbox account holder through their Google Account security settings.
