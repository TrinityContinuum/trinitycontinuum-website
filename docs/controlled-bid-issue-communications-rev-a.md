**INTERNAL / HOLD.** This document supplies ready-to-use communication templates and a release register. It does not authorize external transmission. All bids are contingent on authenticated controlling drawings and written owner release.

## 1. Single-factory GC issue packet assembly
**ONE factory design; THREE qualified general contractors; identical technical and commercial bid issue.** Build one read-only versioned package with:
1. Signed issue cover, bid identifier, revision, deadline/time zone, authorized bid contact, NDA and distribution restrictions.
2. Exhibit A: approved site/survey and jurisdictional requirements.
3. Exhibit B: controlling architectural plans, rooms, kitchen, lab, separate dining/breakroom, showers and lockers.
4. Exhibit C: structural/foundation drawings and equipment load basis.
5. Exhibit D: approved electrical service, one-line, demand and standby requirements.
6. Exhibit E: MEP/process utilities and ventilation.
7. Exhibit F: fire, life safety, hazardous gas/EHS and permitting basis.
8. Exhibit G: factory equipment IDs, OEM/GC split, bay allocations and loads.
9. Exhibit H: production/process flow, external test stands and integration interfaces.
10. Exhibit I: design/procurement/installation responsibility matrix.
11. Exhibit J: milestone schedule and critical path expectations.
12. Exhibit K: inspection, FAT/SAT, commissioning, training and handover criteria.
13. Exhibit L: **manufacturing-facility-gc-bidder-forms-rev-a.md** as the common signed commercial response.
14. Exhibit M: NDA, confidentiality and contract requirements.

**No file is deemed attached merely because listed above.** Fill the controlled issue index with original file name, revision, document owner, approved source, SHA-256, confidentiality and actual inclusion. If any controlling item is missing, retain HOLD and request it from Jack. The historic area, bay, kVA and equipment-budget conflicts are not bidder-selectable design options.

## 4. Common clarification and addendum procedure
- Register each question with timestamp, bidder, exhibit, drawing revision, question, due date, owner and disposition.
- Remove bidder-identifying commercially sensitive material before issuing common technical answers.
- Obtain design-authority approval for any engineering interpretation or change.
- Issue one numbered written addendum with affected source revisions and a replacement-file hash list.
- Distribute each applicable addendum to **all three** approved GCs simultaneously; record delivery and acknowledgment.
- If a change materially alters pricing or schedule, extend the deadline equally for all bidders.
- Never rely on private calls, informal emails or bidder-specific drawing substitutions as the controlling instruction.

**Common addendum template:**
- Bid ID / Addendum number / Date / Supersedes
- Approved technical question and common answer
- Drawings and exhibits changed, old/new revisions, hashes
- Pricing/schedule response required
- Revised common deadline (if any)
- Authorized signature / recipients / receipt acknowledgments

## 5. Bid receipt and completeness review
Record sealed/controlled receipt date, bidder legal entity, signed form, total and reconciled schedule of values, deviations, addenda acknowledgment, bonds/insurance, subcontractors, equipment scope, schedule, warranty, exclusions, and whether the bid is conforming. Request equal-format clarifications where permissible. Do not disclose one bidder's price to another.
