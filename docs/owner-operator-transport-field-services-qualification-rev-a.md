## C. Six-trailer deployment workflow (per approved movement)
1. Trinity approves project, destination, equipment configuration and transport scope.
2. Engineering supplies actual trailer weights, dimensions, center of gravity, securement and handling instructions.
3. Logistics verifies route, bridge/height/weight restrictions, motor-carrier authority, driver credentials, insurance, permits and escort needs.
4. Contractor accepts written scope, rate, fuel/accessorial terms, damage/cargo liability and schedule.
5. Qualified team documents pre-departure inspection, loading, securement, custody and dispatch.
6. Site coordinator receives equipment, checks condition and documents positioning/staging.
7. Only separately qualified and authorized technicians perform utility hookups, energized/pressurized work, commissioning or service.
8. Record acceptance, exceptions, demobilization, invoicing and closeout.
