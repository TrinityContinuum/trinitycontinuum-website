# Trucker-Owned Relocatable Water + Power + Thermal: Exploration Brief
**Status:** Internal decision draft only. No approved product, performance guarantee, financing offer, or external solicitation. **Owner:** Jack Coates. **Relationship:** Separate from the controlled six-trailer Gold flagship.

## Customer problem and proposed role
Explore whether independent truck owners or small fleets could own, transport, lease, or operate relocatable modular infrastructure serving sites with temporary or distributed water, electrical, and useful thermal requirements. Candidate settings include remote construction, industrial turnaround, disaster recovery, agricultural processing, and temporary facilities. None is a validated market yet.

## Alternative arrangements to evaluate
1. **Transport contractor:** Truck owner transports equipment owned and operated by Trinity or a customer. Lowest proposed operational responsibility; assess freight licensing, cargo insurance, loading and unloading, and scheduling.
2. **Equipment owner/lessor:** Owner purchases or finances a compliant mobile asset and leases it to an authorized operator. Assess equipment title, lender security interests, downtime, maintenance reserves, and warranty obligations.
3. **Owner-operator:** Owner transports and operates a modular system under documented training, permits, site agreements, and safety controls. Highest responsibility and potential exposure. No revenue assumption until costs and demand are validated.
4. **Joint venture or revenue share:** Evaluate only after an auditable revenue model, risk allocation, contracts, and legal review. No implied investment offer.

## Engineering and regulatory feasibility questions
- **Mass and dimensions:** Loaded and unloaded axle weights, gross vehicle weight, center of gravity, bridge limits, route restrictions, oversize permits, tie-downs, vibration, and thermal cycling.
- **Water:** Feedwater availability and quality, pretreatment, product-water specifications, potable-water certification where relevant, brine/wastewater discharge, storage, and environmental permissions.
- **Electrical:** Generation source, fuel and emissions, site interconnection/islanding, grounding, protection, electrical inspection, and power-quality requirements.
- **Thermal:** Quantified recoverable heat at specified operating conditions, usable temperature, demand coincidence, piping isolation, and heat rejection when no useful load exists.
- **Site and people:** Footprint, foundations, access, fire separation, noise, vibration, service intervals, staffing, operator training, emergency shutdown, and commissioning.
- **Business:** Utilization, transport distance, setup/teardown labor, consumables, repairs, technicians, insurance, permits, downtime, financing, customer credit, and end-of-life recovery.
