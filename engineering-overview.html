Technical discussion framework

Six trailers, one integrated system

These topics frame the proposed system architecture and associated engineering handoff. Drawings, calculations, vendor qualifications and acceptance evidence remain controlled separately.

01 · System architecture

Six-trailer integrated utility platform

Thermal, power/recovery, vacuum distillation, polishing/storage, and control/auxiliary functions coordinated as a standalone system. Trailer-specific equipment layout requires native CAD verification.

02 · Thermal and energy

Heat recovery and electrical screening

Explain the heat exchangers, energy recovery and below-1-MW best-case screening basis without presenting modeled output as independently proven operational performance.

03 · Water assurance

Treatment, polishing and custody

Discuss pretreatment, vacuum distillation, water-quality instruments, redundant UV finishing and controlled release. Independent sampling and acceptance remain necessary.

04 · Controls and deployment

Autonomous operating architecture

Discuss coordinated controls, safety interlocks, degraded-mode operation, black-start/island capability and integration requirements. Deployment claims must be tied to qualification records.
