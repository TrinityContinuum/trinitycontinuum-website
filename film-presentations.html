Preliminary engineering concepts · November 2026

Proposed Modular Applications

Expansion studies, not built or independently validated installations.

Concept study only · Not for fabrication. Trinity's controlled six-trailer Water + Power + Thermal utility-platform design remains the baseline. The proposed eight-trailer arrangement adds one 53-ft computing module and one 53-ft integrated Blow–Fill–Seal (BFS) water-packaging module. A second computing module would make nine trailers. All additions are subject to engineering, regulatory, vendor and management review.

Proposed trailer 7

Modular computing / data-center study

A 53-ft trailer concept with an 18-rack reference layout and a 20-rack high-density study, using nominal 600-mm racks.

  • At an assumed 40 kW per rack and PUE 1.5: 18 racks model 720 kW IT / approximately 1.08 MW total facility demand; 20 racks model 800 kW IT / approximately 1.20 MW.
  • Rear maintenance access, cooling and heat rejection, power supply, egress, trailer structure and axle loads remain unresolved.
  • Neither rack count is approved as a constructible layout or a verified power-delivery capability.
Proposed trailer 8

Integrated BFS bottled-water study

A single deployable production concept combining potable water finishing, controlled clean processing, blow–fill–seal packaging, inspection and packout.

  • Machine height and cleanroom/service clearances may require an expandable enclosure.
  • Bottle sizes, tooling changeovers, sanitation and throughput depend on OEM-confirmed specifications and approvals.
  • One packaging line is not assumed to bottle Trinity's entire modeled water-production output.

These concepts are additional possible uses for Trinity's integrated utility platform, not current product specifications, certified designs, vendor commitments, or financial projections. Controlled REV01/REV02 handoffs remain internal.

Purpose of the full films and how to view them

The full films add narrated visual context to the four topics above. They are optional materials for attendees to explore after a short meeting or for Jack to open when a participant requests a deeper visual explanation.

The narration tells the story; the illustrations bring the vision to life. Some manufacturing, equipment and deployment imagery repeats, and a given image may illustrate a wider theme rather than the precise words being spoken at that instant.

These are conceptual engineering visualizations, not footage of a commissioned installation or demonstrations of independently verified operating performance. Engineering calculations, native CAD, qualification records and project-specific performance evidence are separate from the films.

Each film is embedded directly above its discussion points. Press play inside the film to watch on this page. Playback requires public HeyGen embed access; no download to Jack’s laptop is needed.
