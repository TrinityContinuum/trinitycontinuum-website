Industrial High-Purity & Ultrapure Water

Modular Water Treatment for Demanding Industrial Applications.

Trinity Continuum is developing trailerized water-treatment and polishing systems intended for data centers, semiconductor and advanced manufacturing facilities, and other industrial applications requiring stringent water quality. Final process configuration depends on incoming feedwater, flow requirements and customer-specific quality specifications. No universal ultrapure-water certification is implied.
