Engineering verification gates

What remains to be demonstrated

Power balance

800 kW modeled IT demand implies approximately 1.2 MW facility demand at illustrative PUE 1.5. That exceeds the separately documented generation best-case below 1 MW. Supplemental supply, staged load or other validated solutions are necessary before full-load claims.

Compute feasibility

Billable vCPU count, processor specifications, oversubscription, utilization, selling rates and customer acquisition are assumptions, not proven commercial capacity or contracted demand.

Water & environmental evidence

30 million gallons/year at 8,000 operating hours is a production target, not tested output or bottled-water sales. Potable finishing, net saleable surplus, permitting, emissions and lifecycle impacts need validation.
