Appointments & virtual meetings

Request a meeting time or join a prearranged face-to-face video conference. No paid account is required to request a meeting.

Request an appointment

Choose a preferred date and time. This sends a request to Trinity by email, not a confirmed reservation. Trinity will reply to confirm availability.

View live booking calendar

Your email program opens with a prepared message. No booking is stored on this website.

If no email application opens, email jack@trinitycontinuum.com directly with your name, reply email, preferred date, time, time zone and discussion topic. Your meeting is not confirmed until Trinity replies.

Join a private Google Meet

For scheduled Trinity Continuum discussions, use the private Google Meet link provided directly by the organizer. The meeting address is never published on this website. A confirmed invitation is required.

Only links from meet.google.com are accepted. The call opens on Google Meet.

Alternative video meeting

Trinity can hold browser-based video calls using Jitsi Meet. For privacy, share a unique room link only with invited participants. Meetings should be arranged with Jack before anyone joins.

Only links on meet.jit.si are accepted. The call opens on Jitsi's service, outside Trinity's website.

For the meeting organizer

Create a hard-to-guess room link and share it privately after agreeing on a time. The public Jitsi service may require the organizer to authenticate, and availability or usage terms can change.

Generating a link does not reserve a room or create a calendar event. Enable Jitsi's lobby or password controls when available.
