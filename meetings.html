Request an appointment

An appointment is booked once the selected time has been placed on Trinity's calendar and a booking confirmation has been issued. Trinity reserves the right to decline, cancel, or reschedule appointments, with notice by email. Until automated booking is activated, this form collects preferred times for confirmation.

Scheduling: Trinity does not offer appointments from 11:30 AM to 1:00 PM Central Time. Other times are requests, not guaranteed availability.

Submit your request directly. Trinity will review the suggested dates and email you a confirmation. Your appointment is not booked until Trinity responds.
