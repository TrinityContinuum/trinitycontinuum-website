Website privacy notice

This static website does not operate an inquiry database or collect inquiry submissions on its server. The inquiry form prepares an email in your email application; any message you choose to send is handled through the email providers involved.

The site uses essential browser features for navigation and interactive diagrams. Google Analytics 4 is optional and loads only after you select Accept analytics. If accepted, Google may receive page paths (without URL query parameters), page views, interaction events, browser/device information and approximate location, and may use analytics cookies. Advertising personalization signals are disabled in the website configuration. If you decline, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. Hosting and network providers may separately process technical access information under their own policies.

Do not send confidential engineering documents, sensitive personal information, or financial records through the public inquiry form. Trinity Continuum management is responsible for confirming its data-retention settings, service-provider arrangements and any applicable regional privacy obligations.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Trinity Continuum. For information about Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy. The website notice should be reviewed whenever analytics settings or site data practices change.
