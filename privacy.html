Website privacy notice

Updated November 25, 2026 — website privacy and internal Google Gmail-connected Coordination Desk

Trinity Continuum offers different types of contact and meeting requests. General project-interest and Gold document-access forms prepare an email for you to review and send through your own email application; those forms do not transmit a message until you send it. The meeting-request form instead sends your name, email address, optional organization, preferred dates and times, time zone and discussion topic to Trinity's private Cloudflare appointment queue and to its Web3Forms email-notification service. Cloudflare Turnstile helps detect automated submissions. A meeting request is not a confirmed booking: Trinity reviews the request and replies before arranging a calendar invitation or video-meeting link. These third-party services may process technical and submitted information as part of delivery.

The site uses essential browser features for navigation and interactive diagrams. Google Analytics 4 is optional and loads only after you select Accept analytics. If accepted, Google may receive page paths (without URL query parameters), page views, interaction events, browser/device information and approximate location, and may use analytics cookies. Advertising personalization signals are disabled in the website configuration. If you decline, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. Hosting and network providers may separately process technical access information under their own policies.

Do not submit confidential engineering documents, sensitive personal information, or financial records through public contact or appointment forms. Trinity Continuum management is responsible for confirming its data-retention settings, service-provider arrangements and any applicable regional privacy obligations.

Google Gmail data used by Trinity Coordination Desk

Trinity Continuum operates a private, company-use Coordination Desk for specifically authorized corporate mailboxes, currently including Mirael and BrotherChat's Trinity addresses. The Desk is an internal read-only email intake and review tool, not a public Gmail integration or email marketing enrollment. Authorized mailbox users grant access through Google's OAuth consent process. The Desk requests gmail.readonly solely to identify and retrieve messages in the authorized mailbox's INBOX and to monitor mailbox-history changes for new or newly inbox-labeled messages.

For permitted company correspondence handling, the Desk temporarily processes email subject and body text in server memory to classify operational items such as meetings, engineering inquiries, procurement, delivery failures and no-contact requests. Its Cloudflare D1 database stores limited technical receipts: which authorized mailbox received the item, opaque Gmail message/thread identifiers, message timestamp, category, urgency, review ownership and status, deduplication cursors and sanitized diagnostic codes. This read-only watcher does not persist email subjects, message bodies or attachments in that D1 receipt store; it does not send messages, delete email, change Gmail flags or authorize external outreach. Classification is a simple rule-based aid requiring separate human or otherwise authorized follow-up. Other Trinity applications and separately connected email clients are not covered by this specific watcher description.

Google user data used by the Desk is processed through Google's Gmail and OAuth services and Trinity's Cloudflare Worker infrastructure solely to perform the described mailbox review and operations. The Desk does not sell Google user data, use it for targeted advertising, or transfer message contents to unrelated recipients. Technical receipts are retained for operational reconciliation and security accountability while needed; a fixed deletion period has not yet been established. Authorized mailbox account holders may request review, deletion or removal of the Desk's access via Trinity's contact page, subject to applicable records obligations. Access can also be revoked through the relevant Google Account's third-party connections. A revoked grant disables future API access, but previously stored technical receipts may require a separate deletion request.

Trinity Continuum's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Security and provider controls should be reassessed when the Desk's functionality, approved users, external processors or retention practices change. For more about this private application, see Trinity Coordination Desk.

For privacy questions or requests, contact Trinity Continuum. For information about Google's processing, see Google's Privacy Policy. The website notice should be reviewed whenever analytics settings or site data practices change.
