Proposed AI reply policy

General inquiries: draft promptly, propose sending within 15–30 minutes.

Project-specific inquiries: 30–60 minutes. Detailed technical inquiries: 60–90 minutes, subject to review requirements.

Pricing, specifications, warranties, procurement commitments and unverified performance claims: hold for management approval.
