Technology overview

Integrated technology

A proposed modular approach to water, power and thermal-energy recovery.

Platform overview

Three linked utility functions.

Trinity's proposed architecture considers electrical generation, water production and recovery of useful thermal energy as a coordinated engineering problem.

This is a conceptual description, not a verified process flow diagram or certified system specification.

Electrical generation

A 1-MW-class design target for the larger platform; the controlled source distinguishes design class from measured or guaranteed electrical output.

Water production

Water-treatment and purification concepts with an approximately 30-million-gallon annual modeled reference for the six-trailer platform.

Thermal recovery

Heat recovery and process integration are central design themes, with integrated thermal balance and equipment qualification still required.

Technical qualification remains open. Historical tests, modeled results, supplier information and independently witnessed integrated results must be identified separately.
