Platform overview

Three linked utility functions.

Trinity's Gold architecture coordinates electrical generation, water production and useful thermal recovery. Existing Trinity component engineering and applicable vendor evidence support the design basis; native installation and integrated acceptance are distinct stages.

The explanatory diagram below is not a P&ID or fabrication drawing. Controlled source drawings govern physical implementation.

Electrical generation

A 1-MW-class design target for the larger platform; the controlled source distinguishes design class from measured or guaranteed electrical output.

Water production

Water-treatment and purification architecture with an approximately 30-million-gallon annual modeled planning reference for the six-trailer platform; Mumbai has its own 4.3–4.5-million-gallon planning basis.

Thermal recovery

Heat recovery is part of the established process architecture. Internal recovery, useful exported heat and residual rejection are reconciled for the applicable installation and operating mode.
