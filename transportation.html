Transportation & Field Services

Trinity Continuum is evaluating a qualified network of independent carriers, truck owner-operators and field-service contractors to support future modular utility deployments.

Opportunities depend on approved projects, equipment transport plans, verified credentials and individually executed contracts. This page does not advertise active loads, guaranteed work or fixed rates.

Carriers & owner-operators

Prospective six-trailer mobilization, delivery, securement, staging, demobilization, relocation and replacement-module logistics, subject to route, weight, dimension and permit review.

Qualified field services

Potential positioning, inspections, authorized hookups, maintenance and modular replacement. Electrical, pressurized, combustion and high-purity water systems require applicable qualifications and Trinity authorization.

Regional coordination

Future capacity planning may include regional dispatch, emergency response, backup carriers, site coordination and verified workforce availability.

Contractor qualification

Prospective carriers should be prepared to provide legal business identity, relevant operating authority, USDOT/MC identifiers where applicable, equipment and trailer-hauling capabilities, driver qualifications, insurance certificates, operating regions and safety/permit experience. Field-service providers should describe applicable trade credentials, insurance, experience and service territory.

Do not send government identification, sensitive employee records, banking details or confidential engineering files through a public contact form. Trinity will identify a controlled exchange process if additional records are needed.

How commercial engagements work

Potential transport arrangements may be priced by trip, loaded mile, mobilization, staging, detention, standby, demobilization, relocation or emergency assignment. Actual rates, fuel/accessorial treatment, insurance allocation, scope and payment terms require written agreement. Technical field services are separately scoped and qualified.

Independent transport or field-service contracts do not confer ownership of Trinity's platforms, intellectual property, utility contracts or water revenue. Nothing on this page is a promise of engagement or a statement that the platform is operational.
